# Research

This directory retains comparative, empirical or methodological material actually used by the experiment. It is not a general reading list and does not contain accepted law.

There are no research records in v0.1.

## Source standard

Prefer primary legal materials, official data and original research when available. A research note should record:

- a stable title and locator;
- author or issuing body when known;
- access date for changeable online material;
- the proposition for which the source is used;
- the source's relevant limits;
- quotation and paraphrase distinctly;
- human and AI involvement in preparing the note.

A source from another jurisdiction is comparative material, not an automatically suitable rule for New Jurisdiction. If a source cannot be verified, label it unverified.

Copy [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) when a retained note is necessary. Do not add unused sources for the appearance of rigor.
