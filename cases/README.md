# Cases

Cases are synthetic fact patterns used to test a proposal or accepted rule. They expose ambiguity, boundary conditions, conflicting interests and unwanted consequences.

Cases are not real disputes, precedent, evidence of real-world effects or accepted law.

There are no case records in v0.1.

## File format

Copy [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) to `NJ-C-####.md`. Use the next unused identifier and replace every instruction.

A useful case states:

- the facts and expressly assumed facts;
- the legal question;
- the provisions or proposal being tested;
- the expected application and competing application;
- the failure or uncertainty the case exposes;
- who or what authored the case and any material AI involvement.

Do not write a case only to make a preferred proposal look successful. Include difficult cases and plausible adverse interpretations.
