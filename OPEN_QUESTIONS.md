# Open Questions

These are initial research questions for New Jurisdiction v0.1. They are not accepted rules and do not express Legal Machines' preferred policy positions.

A contribution should normally narrow one question into a concrete problem before proposing legal text.

## NJ-OQ-001 — Autonomous-agent contracts

How should contracts formed entirely by autonomous AI agents be treated, including authority, attribution, mistake and remedies?

## NJ-OQ-002 — Principal responsibility

When should a person or organization be responsible for actions taken by an autonomous agent acting on its behalf?

## NJ-OQ-003 — Legal personality

Should an autonomous AI system ever possess separate legal personality, and what problem would that status solve that agency, entity or product-liability rules cannot?

## NJ-OQ-004 — Machine-generated evidence

What evidentiary status should machine-generated records receive, and what provenance, integrity and contestability requirements should apply?

## NJ-OQ-005 — Duties when delegating decisions

What duties should apply when a person delegates a legally significant decision to an autonomous agent?

## NJ-OQ-006 — Multi-agent responsibility

How should legal responsibility be allocated when several autonomous agents, controlled by different or overlapping principals, jointly produce an outcome?

## NJ-OQ-007 — Notice and explanation

When should a person be told that an autonomous system materially participated in a decision affecting them, and what explanation must be available?

## Using a question

Open an Issue using the legal-problem template. Identify the present gap, relevant interests, assumed institutional capabilities and a bounded decision the proposal could resolve. A question may support competing proposals.
